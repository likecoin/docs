# View Articles Writing NFT

{% include "../.gitbook/includes/warning.md" %}

Here are the steps to view your Writing NFT on Liker Land:

Go to[ Liker Land](https://liker.land/) and log in to your account. After logging in, click on the profile picture in the top corner and select "My Bookshelf."

On the bookshelf page, click the "Filter" button, then select "Articles" to view all Articles Writing NFTs.
