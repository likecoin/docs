# 新書上架

到 [Liker Land Book Press](https://publish.liker.land/) 網站，進入網站後按左下角「Sign in」連結錢包，再點「[Latest Books](https://publish.liker.land/latest-books)」。

### Latest Books on Liker Land Bookstore

如未進行用戶設定，系統會顯示「Join Our Affiliation Program! Setup your Liker ID and Stripe Connect Account to participate in sales affiliation program on Liker Land Bookstore.」，請至[用戶設定](user/)完成推廣聯盟計劃設定。

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Latest Books 1.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Latest Books</p></figcaption></figure>

完成設定後出現「Append ?from=@`[你的 Liker ID]` in any bookstore page to earn commission from book sales」字樣。點「Copy」複製新書推廣連結。再將它發送開去，賺取推廣佣金。

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Latest Books 2.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>點「Copy」</p></figcaption></figure>
